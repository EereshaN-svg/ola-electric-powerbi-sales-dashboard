# 🚀 Ola Electric Sales Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Ola Electric sales performance and provide meaningful business insights from sales data.

The dashboard focuses on revenue, units sold, profitability, product performance, regional performance, payment methods, and dealer performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track revenue and revenue growth
- Monitor units sold
- Analyze profit and profit margin
- Identify top-performing models
- Compare regional revenue
- Analyze payment methods
- Evaluate dealer performance
- Compare monthly unit sales across years
- Provide interactive filtering for business analysis

## 📌 Dashboard KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total Units Sold
- Average Selling Price
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Revenue YoY Growth

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top 5 Models by Revenue
- Revenue by Region
- Units Sold by Month
- Payment Type Analysis
- Top 5 Dealers by Revenue
- Interactive slicers for Year, Quarter, Month, State, and Model
